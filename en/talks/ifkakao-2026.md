I took part as a **panelist** in a fireside chat at **if(kakao)26**, Kakao's developer conference.
Three panelists from Kakao, Kakao Pay Insurance, and AWS sat down with a moderator to talk about
what each of us decided so that we can keep using AI at work.

  <li><b>Event</b>: if(kakao)26 (Oct 13 - 14, 2026, Kakao AI Campus, Yongin)</li>

  <li><b>Track</b>: Developers in the AI era</li>
  <li><b>Date</b>: October 13, 2026, 15:00 - 16:00</li>
  <li><b>Venue</b>: B3F Growing hall</li>
  <li><b>Moderator</b>: ice.o (오영은), DevEx lead at Kakao</li>
