I took part as a **panelist** in the crew panel talk at **if(kakao)AI 2024**, Kakao's developer
conference. Four crew members shared how their careers are changing in the AI era, the doubts that
come with that shift, and what they learned applying the technology to real work. The session ran
live on the outdoor stage for 52 minutes.

  <li><b>Event</b> — if(kakao)AI 2024 (Oct 22 - 24, 2024, kakao AI campus)</li>

  <li><b>Date</b> — October 24, 2024, 13:00 - 14:00</li>
  <li><b>Venue</b> — Event Stage, Team ground (52-minute live outdoor session)</li>
